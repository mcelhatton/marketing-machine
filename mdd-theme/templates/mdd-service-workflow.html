How it works

RTLS knows where the car is.

Other tools wait for someone to log that a car left the bay. MDD already knows — so the status is always accurate.

01

Tag at Arrival

Every vehicle and key gets a tracking tag the moment it hits the drive. Nothing for advisors to babysit.

02

Stages Advance

As the car moves from write-up to bay to QC to ready, each step logs itself — nothing to forget, nothing to game.

03

See It at a Glance

The hardware

Reusable placards, not paper hang tags.

Paper hang tags are single-use, easy to lose, and tell you nothing. MDD's durable, color-coded placards clip on at write-up — each with a built-in tracker that reports the exact location of the vehicle and its key in real time. Reuse them for years.

Color-code by status, lane, or department for an at-a-glance read across the drive.

Easy to store, fast to grab

Store them easily. Grab them in seconds.

Keep your placards on a wall rack, in a cabinet, or at the tire racks — wherever's handy. They store neatly and are quick to grab and clip into a customer's vehicle. And because they replace single-use paper hang tags, dealers save about $500 a month on average.
