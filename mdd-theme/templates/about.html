Why We Exist

A dealership isn't a fleet.

For years, dealers who wanted to find a vehicle had one option: GPS — a tool borrowed from long-haul trucking. It goes blind indoors, can't tell which floor of a garage a car is on, doesn't live on your team's phones, and never tracked the one thing that stops a sale cold: the key.

Dealers didn't need a better trucking tool. They needed something built from the ground up for how a dealership actually works — cars and keys, indoors and out, down to the stall.

One Year, One Impossible Lot

We built it at the largest dealership in the world.

Our founder spent all of 2015 building MDD inside Longo Toyota — roughly 50 acres, a six-story parking structure, and 90+ service bays. If a real-time location system could survive that, it could work anywhere. More than ten years later, Longo is still a customer — and the same platform now tracks heavy equipment through Case New Holland manufacturing plants.
