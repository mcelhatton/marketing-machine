## The Technology: How It Works

**Key Concept:** The Bluetooth tags aren't just for finding things. They continuously generate location data that becomes the foundation for workflows, reporting, analytics, and integrations. MDD is building the operational intelligence layer for dealerships.

### The Basic Flow

1. **Tag** — Every vehicle and key gets a Bluetooth tracker. Staff scans the QR code on the tag and assigns it to the VIN. Takes ~10 seconds.

2. **Track** — Real-time location visible on mobile app (iOS/Android) or desktop dashboard. Pinpoint accuracy across multiple lots.

3. **Manage** — Run audits, set movement alerts, see who last had a key, integrate with DMS.

4. **Analyze** — The continuous location data powers reporting: floorplan verification, lot rot alerts, off-lot accountability, dwell time analytics, and more.

### Bluetooth vs. GPS

MDD uses **Bluetooth, not GPS**. This is important because:
- GPS doesn't work well indoors
- Dealership vehicles are often inside (service bays, detail shops, covered lots)
- Bluetooth provides accurate tracking where GPS fails

### DMS Integration

MDD integrates with major Dealer Management Systems:
- Reynolds & Reynolds
- CDK

Stock numbers and VINs auto-populate. No manual data entry.

### LocateIQ — The Unique Mechanism

**LocateIQ** (pronounced "locate IQ") is MDD's proprietary workflow automation technology.

**How it's different:**
Traditional workflow software relies on staff manually updating the system when a car moves from one stage to another. Staff forget. Dashboards become inaccurate.

LocateIQ tracks the **physical location** of every vehicle. When a car physically moves (from service bay to detail shop, for example), the workflow updates automatically. No manual entry required.

**Result:** Workflow dashboards that are actually accurate. Bottlenecks identified in real-time. No reliance on staff remembering to log changes.

LocateIQ powers both Service Workflow and Recon Workflow products.

## Appendix: Industry Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| CSI | Customer Satisfaction Index — OEM metric, often tied to pay |
| RO | Repair Order — service department work order |
| F&I | Finance & Insurance — where add-on products are sold |
| DMS | Dealer Management System (Reynolds & Reynolds, CDK) |
| Recon | Reconditioning — preparing used cars for sale |
| Frontline | Ready for sale on the lot |
| Bay turns | Number of vehicles processed through a service bay |
| Floorplan | Financing arrangement where bank owns inventory; dealer pays interest until sold |
| Lot rot | Vehicles sitting on lot too long without selling — aging inventory |
| Dwell time | How long a vehicle sits at a particular stage or location |
| 20 Group | Peer advisory boards for dealership owners |
| NADA | National Automobile Dealers Association |
| NCM Associates | Popular 20 Group moderator |
| FOD | Fixed Operations Director (oversees service) |
