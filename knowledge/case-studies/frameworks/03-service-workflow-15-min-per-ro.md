# Case Study Outline: 15 Minutes Saved Per Repair Order

**Product:** Service Workflow (powered by LocateIQ)
**Angle:** Service Revenue — bay turns and cycle time. More billable hours through efficiency.
**Target personas:** Service Manager (primary), GM, Owner
**Follow:** `00-master-case-study-framework.md` for structure, voice, SEO, and checklist.

---

## Story Arc
A service department was losing minutes on every RO — techs walking the lot to find the next car, advisors guessing at status, dashboards that didn't match reality. Service Workflow gave back 15 minutes per RO, and those minutes compound into real billable hours and bay turns.

## Candidate Proof Points (user selects final customer)
- **Longo Toyota** — 15 minutes saved per RO; vehicle location 25 minutes → seconds; 3 days saved per car in recon. Quote: "MDD delivers on its promises. Locating a vehicle went from 25 minutes to seconds. Every dealer needs these results." — Mark Seipel, Fixed Operations Director.
- **Brandon Honda** — 41 minutes saved per RO (if the user wants the bigger number).
- **Corwin Toyota** — 64% of customers out in 60 min; 96% in 90 min; 40-60% bay turn increase (if wait-time framing preferred).
- Supporting aggregate: 25% repair cycle time reduction.
- Expert quote: "Service managers often focus on stacking work into each repair order. But cycle time management looks at how to add more billable hours through efficiency." — Ed French, President, AutoProfit Group.

**Challenge — lead with the invisible minutes:**
- Hook: a tech finishes a job, and the next 15 minutes disappear — walking the lot for the next car, hunting the key, asking who moved it.
- Pains: minutes lost on every single RO, multiplied by hundreds of ROs a month; the workflow dashboard "looks organized — but is it accurate?"; someone has to remember to log that a car left the bay, and they often don't; CSI at risk (tied to Service Manager pay and OEM sponsorship).
- Do the math for the reader: 15 min × {{ROs/month}} = {{hours}} of shop time per month.

**Solution:**
- Service Workflow powered by **LocateIQ** — because MDD knows where the car physically is, the workflow advances automatically when the vehicle moves. No manual status updates.
- Features: end-to-end visibility drop-off → delivery, real-time alerts on slow-moving jobs, cycle time reporting, bay turn tracking, dashboard filtering by stage/tech/time-in-process.
- Include the 40-minute-mark idea if using Corwin: know early whether a delivery promise is at risk.

**Results table candidates:** Time per RO → 15 min saved | Vehicle location time → seconds | Bay turns → {{after}} | Cycle time → {{after}} | CSI → improved.

**Why It Worked (VS block):** Traditional workflow tools (manual status logging, dashboard accuracy depends on staff discipline, bottlenecks found after the fact) vs. Service Workflow + LocateIQ (auto-advances on physical movement, always-accurate board, proactive alerts).
