# Case Study Outline: Lot Management

**Product:** MDD Locate (vehicle tracking / lot management)
**Angle:** Saturday Chaos — "Customer ready to buy. Key missing. Sale stalls." Plus scale story.
**Target personas:** Owner, GM, Sales Manager, Used Car Manager
**Follow:** `00-master-case-study-framework.md` for structure, voice, SEO, and checklist.

---

## Story Arc
A high-volume dealership couldn't find cars across multiple lots — salespeople walking rows, customers waiting, deals dying. With MDD Locate, every vehicle's position is visible instantly, at enterprise scale.

## Candidate Proof Points (user selects final customer)
- **Bill Brown Ford** (world's largest Ford dealership) — 2,000+ vehicles tracked across multiple lots; 90% adoption across the 29-person new car sales team; lots of 800/1,000/1,700 units. Quote: "Mobile Dealer Data allows us to instantly locate the exact position of 2,000 vehicles spread across several lots…" — Dave Bird, New Car Inventory Manager.
- Supporting aggregate: 85% reduction in vehicle location time.
- **Longo Toyota** — "Locating a vehicle went from 25 minutes to seconds." — Mark Seipel, Fixed Operations Director.

**Challenge — lead with the Saturday scenario:**
- Hook: "It's Saturday at 11:30. The showroom is packed. A customer wants to test drive a specific vehicle — but nobody knows where it's parked."
- Pains: cars spread across multiple lots and rows; dealer trades and overflow lots off-site; salespeople walking the lot instead of selling; test drives delayed; deal momentum dying while the customer checks the next dealership's app.
- What they tried: paper lot maps, DMS stock notes, "ask around" — all stale the moment a porter moves a car.

**Solution:**
- MDD Locate: Bluetooth tag on every vehicle. Tag → Track → Manage.
- Features to highlight: real-time location across all properties, DMS integration (stock numbers/VINs auto-populate — Reynolds & Reynolds, CDK), daily audits without walking the lot, after-hours movement alerts (geofenced).
- Mechanism: physical location, always current — no one has to remember to log where a car got parked.

**Results table candidates:** Time to locate a vehicle → seconds | Vehicles under tracking → {{N}} | Sales team adoption → {{%}} | Daily lot audit → minutes.

**Why It Worked (VS block):** Manual processes / stale lot maps (accurate only until the next porter move, deals wait on searches) vs. MDD Locate (live position of every unit, delivery-ready without delays, audits in minutes).

**Quote slot:** Scale quote (2,000 vehicles) for enterprise credibility, or speed quote (25 min → seconds).
