## Story Arc
A dealership was quietly bleeding money on lost keys — replacements, wasted search time, stalled deals — with no visibility into how bad it was. After MDD Locate: **zero lost keys**.

## Candidate Proof Points (user selects final customer)
- **Brandon Honda** — zero lost keys after MDD; also 41 min saved per RO.
- **Bill Brown Ford** — key accountability quote: "MDD allows me to verify if the key control system is securing the vehicle key or if it has been taken by an individual. We can trace it back to someone's desk or the lot." — Dave Bird, New Car Inventory Manager.
- Supporting aggregate: 90% key retrieval time reduction.

## Section Guidance

**Hero H1 direction:** `How {{DEALERSHIP}} Went from Losing Keys Weekly to Zero`

**Stats bar (pick 3):** Zero lost keys | $300-$800 saved per avoided replacement | {{search-time metric}} | 90% faster key retrieval (aggregate).

**Challenge — lead with the quiet bleed:**
- Hook: "Do you know how many keys your dealership replaces every year?" Modern fobs run $300-$800 each.
- Pains: replacement invoices nobody tracks; 3 employees searching the lot; deals stalling while customers wait; keys on desks, in pockets, in the wrong drawer.
- What they tried: legacy key management (lock boxes) — "only as good as the last person that took the key." Accountability, not location.

**Solution:**
- MDD Locate: Bluetooth tracker on every key. Tag (scan QR, assign to VIN, 10 seconds) → Track (real-time location from phone or desktop) → Manage (audits, who-last-had-it accountability).
- Mechanism: lock boxes tell you who took the key last; MDD shows where the key is right now — on a desk, in a pocket, across the lot. Bluetooth works indoors where cars actually are.

**Results table candidates:** Lost keys per month/year → 0 | Key replacement spend → $0 | Time to locate a key → seconds | Daily audit time → {{after}}.

**Why It Worked (VS block):** Legacy key management (accountability only, keys still vanish, no location) vs. MDD Locate (real-time location, who-had-it trail, instant audits).
